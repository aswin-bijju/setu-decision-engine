What the review says

Our reading: simpler origin procedures go with higher preference use in most settings studied, and the effect is largest for small exporters. None of the studies covers India, so the size of the effect for Client A’s clients is open.
No study measures uptake of exporter statements for shipments into India. This is a gap in the literature, not an error in the search.
