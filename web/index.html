How the engine works

  1. Observe10 sources: CRM, calendar, calls, Slack, email, buying signals, financial reports, product usage, hiring, news
  2. UnderstandEntity resolution to account, deal, stakeholder and competitor
  3. ReasonForecast, Pipeline, Champion, Competition, Territory, Expansion and Corridor agents, then Manager and Executive
  4. PrioritiseImpact × urgency × strategic fit × confidence × learned agent weight
  5. RecommendA short queue with evidence, counter-signals and confidence
  6. ReviewApprove, adjust or reject. Approved actions write back to CRM and Slack
  7. LearnEach verdict moves the owning agent's weight
